### 2. Google My Business (10 min)
**Why**: Appear in local search results and Google Maps

**Steps**:

6. Verify your business

**Result**: Increased visibility in local searches

## 💡 Quick Tips for Each Business Page

Your business pages should have:

1. **Title Format**:
   ```
   {Business Name} - {Service} in {City} | CHWK
   ```
   Example: "Jain Home Service - Plumbing in Mumbai | CHWK"

2. **Description Format** (155-160 chars):
   ```
   {Business Name} offers {services} in {location}. ⭐ {rating} stars • {reviews} reviews • Verified • Call: {phone}
   ```

3. **Content Structure**:
   - H1: Business Name
   - H2: About, Services, Location, Reviews, Contact
   - H3: Sub-sections

4. **Must-Have Elements**:
   - ✅ Cover image (done!)
   - ✅ Business logo
   - ✅ Contact information
   - ✅ Opening hours
   - ✅ Reviews and ratings
   - ✅ Photos (minimum 10)
   - ✅ Map location

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Keyword Stuffing**: Don't repeat keywords unnaturally
2. ❌ **Buying Links**: Google will penalize you
3. ❌ **Duplicate Content**: Each page must be unique
4. ❌ **Slow Site**: Must load in under 3 seconds
5. ❌ **No Mobile Version**: 60% of traffic is mobile
6. ❌ **Ignoring Analytics**: Track everything!
7. ❌ **Set and Forget**: SEO needs continuous work
8. ❌ **Black Hat Tactics**: Stay ethical
9. ❌ **Thin Content**: Quality over quantity
10. ❌ **No Local SEO**: Critical for directory sites
