## ⚡ Quick Wins (Do Today!)

1. **Google Search Console** (10 min)
   - Verify your site
   - Submit sitemap
   - Check for errors

2. **Google My Business** (10 min)
   - Create profile
   - Add business info
   - Upload photos

3. **Page Speed** (5 min)
   - Test at pagespeed.web.dev
   - Fix critical issues
   - Aim for 90+ score

4. **Meta Tags** (15 min)
   - Verify all pages have unique titles
   - Check descriptions (155-160 chars)
   - Ensure proper H1 tags

5. **Directory Submit** (10 min)
   - Submit to Google My Business
   - Submit to Bing Places
   - Submit to JustDial

**Total Time: 50 minutes for huge SEO boost!**
